# The Ultimate Book Tracker

### The Problem
* You buy a book 
* You read exactly twelve pages
* You put it on a shelf and completely forget about it
* Six months later, you forget how many pages you read, what the book was about, and if you even liked it
* Basically, human brains are terrible at remembering things

### The Solution
* A phone app that forces you to keep track of your reading habits
* You tell the app what book is currently on your nightstand
* Every time you finish a reading session, you type in the page number you stopped at
* The app does all the math and tells you exactly how much of a reading champion you are

### The Cool Stuff Inside
* The Bookmark Memory: It remembers your exact page so you do not have to fold the corners of your books like a monster
* The Consistency Grid: A giant wall of squares that turns green when you read. If you get lazy and miss a day, it stays gray and judges you silently
* Night Owl Mode: A dark theme toggle because staring at a bright white screen at two in the morning is a terrible idea

### How We Built It (The Tech Stack)
* React Native and Expo: The magic code that makes the app look and feel like a real phone app instead of a boring website
* Node and Express: The brain sitting on a server far away, taking your page numbers and figuring out what to do with them
* MongoDB: The giant digital filing cabinet where all your book titles and reading stats live permanently

### How To Run This On Your Computer

* Step 1: Wake up the Brain (The Backend)
  1. Open your terminal and go into the backend folder
  2. Type npm install and hit Enter (this downloads all the required parts)
  3. Type npm run dev and hit Enter. The server is now running and listening for data

* Step 2: Wake up the App (The Frontend)
  1. Open a second terminal window and go into the frontend folder
  2. Type npm install and hit Enter
  3. Type npx expo start and hit Enter

* Step 3: See it Live
  * Once the Expo menu pops up in the terminal, you have two choices:
  * Press w on your keyboard to instantly open the app in your computer's web browser
  * Download the Expo Go app on your phone, scan the giant QR code on the screen, and the app will magically load right onto your phone
